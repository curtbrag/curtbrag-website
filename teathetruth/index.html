The Artist

Built Different

Teathetruth is an artist, producer, and engineer out of Bankblock Studios in Lynn, Massachusetts. Everything is done in-house — writing, production, recording, mixing, mastering. No middlemen. No shortcuts.

With roots tied to Greece, the music pulls from two worlds — the grit of Lynn and the soul of Athens. Projects like Santorini, 1 Am On Kallithea, and Greek Corner were born between both coasts of the Atlantic.

Beyond the music, Tea is a visual artist — painting and drawing with the same raw, unfiltered honesty that drives every beat. Family, legacy, and lived truth are the foundation. UNCLE TEA is the latest proof.
