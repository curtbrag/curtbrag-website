The Artist

Built Different

Teathetruth is an artist, producer, and engineer out of Lynn, Massachusetts. Everything is done in-house — writing, production, recording, mixing, mastering. No middlemen. No shortcuts. 28 releases since 2020 and counting.

With roots tied deep to Greece, the music pulls from two worlds — the grit of Lynn and the soul of Athens. Santorini, Santorini II, 1 Am On Kallithea, Greek Corner, Helios, The Oddysey — these projects were born between both coasts of the Atlantic. Tea's recorded at Blk Cld Studio in Athens, collaborating with Greek artists Vikkie and sarkitoo.

Beyond the music, Tea is a visual artist — painting and drawing with the same raw, unfiltered honesty that drives every beat. Dark canvases, gold light, expressionist strokes. Family, legacy, and lived truth are the foundation. UNCLE TEA is the latest proof.
